About Our Community

Room For Thought is a small community gathering centered around intentional conversations.


Each event brings together a small group of people to explore a question, concept, or theme from different perspectives.


Participants are encouraged to bring stories, observations, quotes, passages from books, film clips, articles, or personal experiences that relate to the topic. The goal is not debate or persuasion, but thoughtful exploration and genuine human connection.


Based in Burnaby, British Columbia, Room For Thought is open to the public. Anyone is welcome to join by registering for an upcoming event.
